The Problem

Information is buried.

Important details in PDFs are often scattered across tables, hidden in footnotes, buried in charts, appended in long documents, or obscured across different versions.

Conflicting numbers and scanned pages make manual review tedious and error-prone. PDF Insight turns this complexity into structured intelligence.

Core Capabilities

A rigorous layer between documents and decisions.

01

Evidence-backed analysis

Verify important numbers against the source. Don't rely on black-box summaries.

02

Quantitative verification

Check totals, percentages, rates, growth, ratios, deltas, and variances.

03

Compare documents

Identify material changes, contradictions, and new risks across versions.

04

OCR-aware analysis

05

Decision dashboards

Turn findings into KPIs, risks, actions, and decisions.

06

Source mapping

Trace findings back to document, page, section, table, or figure seamlessly.

Workflow

How it works.

01 — Inspect

Extract structure

Maps tables, figures, definitions, dates, units, and qualifiers across the entire document.

02 — Verify

Check claims

Test important quantitative claims and identify inconsistencies within the data.

03 — Synthesize

Connect evidence

Link information across the document or between multiple documents to form a complete picture.

04 — Decide

Produce findings

Deliver a clear decision view with findings, risks, recommended actions, and decision-ready outputs.
