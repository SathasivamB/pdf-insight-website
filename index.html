From document to decision

Stop reading PDFs as isolated pages. Start reading them as evidence.

Important decisions often depend on scattered figures, footnotes, tables, qualifiers, definitions, and version changes. PDF Insight connects those pieces so the conclusions stay traceable to the source.

Capabilities

A rigorous layer between documents and decisions.

01

Evidence-backed analysis

Trace findings to precise documents, pages, sections, tables, and figures instead of relying on vague summaries.

02

Verify the numbers

Check totals, percentages, rates, growth, deltas, ratios, and variances while flagging missing inputs and denominator changes.

03

Compare documents

Identify material changes, contradictions, changed assumptions, and what those differences mean for a decision.

04

Decision dashboards

Turn evidence into structured KPIs, findings, risks, actions, and decisions without inventing unsupported details.

05

OCR-aware review

Handle scanned and imperfect documents with explicit awareness of extraction quality and unresolved evidence.

06

Report-ready outputs

Produce executive briefs, analytical reports, research summaries, audit findings, risk trackers, and comparison reports.

Workflow

Inspect. Verify. Synthesize. Decide.

01

Inspect

Map headings, pages, tables, figures, definitions, dates, qualifiers, footnotes, and document metadata.

02

Verify

Test quantitative claims and distinguish source facts from calculations, synthesis, and unresolved questions.

03

Synthesize

Connect evidence across sections or documents and identify contradictions, changes, risks, and implications.

04

Decide

Deliver a clear decision view with findings, risks, recommended actions, and confidence levels.
