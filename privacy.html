Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

PDF Insight is a document-analysis service made available through ChatGPT. This policy explains how the PDF Insight service is designed to handle information. It does not replace or modify the privacy, retention, or data-control policies that apply to your ChatGPT account or workspace.

Information processed

When you use PDF Insight, the service may receive the PDF documents and instructions that you provide in your ChatGPT conversation, together with information needed to produce the requested analysis. PDF Insight does not operate its own document-storage database or maintain a separate user document library.

Uploaded PDFs and ChatGPT

PDF Insight operates within ChatGPT. Uploaded files may be stored and managed by ChatGPT according to the features, account settings, plan, and workspace policies that apply to you. ChatGPT may save uploaded files to its Library when that feature is available. Deleting a ChatGPT conversation does not necessarily delete a separately saved Library file; users can manage saved files through ChatGPT's Library.

For current details about ChatGPT file storage and retention, see OpenAI's ChatGPT Library guidance and Chat and file retention guidance.

Data use and model improvement

PDF Insight does not independently control whether content submitted through ChatGPT is eligible for model improvement. That is determined by the applicable ChatGPT service, workspace, and data-control settings. Users should review their ChatGPT Data Controls and workspace policies for the settings that apply to them.

Third-party services

PDF Insight does not currently connect your uploaded PDFs to a separate external storage, analytics, advertising, payment, or customer-data platform. The service is delivered through ChatGPT and therefore relies on the systems and policies of the platform hosting the experience.

Support communications

If you contact PDF Insight support, your message and any information you choose to include may be processed for the purpose of responding to your request and improving support. Please avoid sending sensitive document contents unless they are necessary to resolve the issue.

Security

PDF Insight does not operate a separate PDF repository. Security and retention for files handled by ChatGPT are governed by the applicable ChatGPT or workspace controls. You should use the security and data-control features available in your ChatGPT account or organization.

Your choices

You can manage or delete eligible files and conversations through ChatGPT. Workspace users may also be subject to organization-level retention and security controls. If you have a question about PDF Insight's handling of support requests, contact sathasivamb95@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
